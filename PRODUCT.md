# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative professionals and video production teams — AI engineers, video editors, marketing producers, content creators — people who make or commission moving image work. They are skilled at their craft but time-pressed. They land on the site to evaluate whether Higgsfield can genuinely accelerate or replace parts of their pipeline. Many arrive from referrals or social (Twitter, Discord, YouTube demos). They skim fast, judge credibility on first visual impression, and leave within seconds if the site feels generic or uninformed.

## Product Purpose

Higgsfield AI is a professional creative studio in the browser — 40+ AI-powered tools for image generation, video creation, cinematic direction, VFX, voice synthesis, agent-based workflows, and rendering, all coordinated through a single interface. The landing page must communicate range without chaos, credibility without coldness, and power without overwhelming a first-time visitor. Its job is to turn a curious scroller into a person who clicks into a studio.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, precise, confident. Not a hype machine. Not a toy. Think professional camera equipment — every surface feels deliberate, the palette is controlled, the typography has weight. Understated luxury for AI creativity.

## Anti-references

- Any site that leads with a video auto-playing at full volume.
- SaaS cream — off-white backgrounds, rounded corners everywhere, everything floating on shadow cards.
- Gradient text as a primary visual element.
- "AI-powered" repeated in every headline like a keyword spasm.
- Stock photos of diverse hands holding tablets.
- AI-generated abstract shapes as background decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Surface tells the story.** Color, spacing, and type carry meaning. Nothing is arbitrary decoration.
2. **Range through restraint.** 40+ tools communicated as an organized grid, not a firehose of badges. Trust the user to drill in.
3. **Motion as punctuation.** Animate what needs emphasis. Silence is the default. Only move what helps.
4. **Copy that earns its weight.** Every label and description either names a tool or states its outcome. No filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA across the landing page. Reduced-motion respected. Color contrast minimum 4.5:1 on all text. Keyboard navigation on all interactive elements. No hover-only interaction patterns.
